Selected research

IEEE BIBM 2026 · Accepted

Spatiotemporal Collaborative Learning for Angle-Closure Progression Assessment Using Dynamic AS-OCT Videos

Yinglin Zhang*, Jialin Li*, Lingxi Zeng*, Hengquan Guo, Risa Higashita, and Jiang Liu

SCL-Net couples iris-motion representations from a video foundation model with local angle structures through bidirectional gated queries and adaptive decision fusion.

Evaluated on a dataset of 1,308 half-angle videos from 206 subjects (327 eyes), with eye-level train/validation/test separation. SCL-Net achieved 85.6% balanced accuracy on the 256-video, three-class test set.

IEEE TPAMI · Under review

Anatomy-Preserving Structural Evolution Modeling for Physically Plausible Medical Image Prediction

Yinglin Zhang, Xiangyun Zheng, Jingtian Huang, Jialin Li, Risa Higashita, Keiichiro Okamoto, Kazutaka Kamiya, Kazunori Miyata, Akihito Igarashi, Seiichiro Hata, Tomoaki Nakamura, and Jiang Liu

APSevo couples Hierarchical Structural Evolution (HSE) learning with Anatomy-Preserving Representation Transport (APT) and Change-Aware Detail (CAD) preservation to predict target anatomical states.

The manuscript evaluates three prediction tasks: ICL postoperative prediction, retinal treatment-response prediction, and cardiac motion prediction.

MICCAI OMIA 2025

DVIA-Net: Dual-path Video Information Aggregation Network for Anterior Chamber Angle Analysis

Lingxi Zeng*, Yinglin Zhang*, Jialin Li, Xiaoli Xing, Lingxi Hu, Chenglin Yao, Tianhang Liu, Yi Yue, Zunjie Xiao, Chen Lin, Risa Higashita, and Jiang Liu

Combines iris motion with stationary angle structures from dark/light key frames to distinguish open, appositional, and synechial angles.

Paper

Evaluated on 1,304 half-angle videos from 205 patients (326 eyes), with eye-level train/validation/test separation. DVIA-Net achieved 82.2% balanced accuracy on the 256-video test set.

IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics · 2025

Online Bayesian Approximation Based Uncertainty Aware Model for Ophthalmic Image Segmentation

Yinglin Zhang, Risa Higashita, Lingxi Zeng, Jialin Li, Ruiling Xi, Tianhang Liu, Huazhu Fu, Dave Towey, Ruibin Bai, and Jiang Liu

OBU-Net updates spatial uncertainty maps during training to focus on ambiguous regions and fuse multi-scale segmentation predictions using pixel-wise confidence.

Paper

Evaluated on six ophthalmic datasets: TM-EM3000, Rodrep, CORN-1, GOALS, FIVES, and MSCAS2. The study also examines robustness through metamorphic testing and uses an image-level uncertainty score to assess segmentation reliability.
